Ingredients

  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil or Butter
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Equipment

  • Spatula
  • Frying Pan

Directions

  1. 1. Beat the Eggs until thoroughly combined.
  2. 2. Gently preheat pan over medium heat, using either olive oil or a little butter in it.
  3. 3. Cook the mixed eggs in the pan over the course of 5 minutes while making sure to fold and stir the eggs with a spatula.
